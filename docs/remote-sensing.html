Seasonal dynamics of meadow condition

Four surveys conducted between June and September 2026 provide paired RGB orthomosaics and NDVI maps for the stable and unstable meadows.

View the RGB and NDVI time series →

The maps show a seasonal increase in NDVI from June towards late summer in both meadow areas. Higher NDVI values form a more continuous spatial pattern in the stable meadow, whereas the unstable meadow retains a more heterogeneous distribution. In June, NDVI shows heterogeneous patterns in both areas, where NDVI is higher in permanently submerged area (channels, pounds…). By September, elevated NDVI occupies substantial portions of both areas, consistent with the late-summer development of the meadow and the timing of the phenological maximum. In September, the meadow of the stable area shows homogeneous NDVI values almost across its entire extent whereas the meadow of the unstable area still shows the same patterns as in June, with higher NDVI value in wet area and lower in areas that dry during low tide.

Image-derived seagrass cover

Field photographs collected in September 2026 provided sample-scale estimates of Zostera noltei percentage cover for the subsequent calibration of drone-derived NDVI. Percentage cover varied among the 24 samples, reflecting marked spatial heterogeneity in the amount of vegetation present within the sampled areas. The complete circular sampling area was visible in every photograph.

NDVI–seagrass cover relationship

Mean September NDVI was calculated within a 10 cm radius of each sample position. Across the 24 samples, NDVI ranged from 0.190 to 0.605 and showed a positive association with photograph-derived seagrass percentage cover. A bounded logistic calibration gave the following empirical relationship:

\[ \mathrm{SPC} = \frac{100}{1 + \exp\!\left(2.134 - 3.927\,\mathrm{NDVI}\right)}, \]

where SPC is seagrass percentage cover. The model explained 59.6% of the observed variation (\(R^2 = 0.596\)), with a root-mean-square error of 7.80 percentage points. The calibration is supported over the observed NDVI interval and should not be extrapolated beyond this range.

Temporal NDVI at experimental cores

Mean NDVI at the experimental cores increased from June to September in both meadow areas, although the magnitude of this seasonal increase differed markedly between them. In June, mean NDVI was lower in the stable meadow than in the unstable meadow (0.203 and 0.262, respectively). The trajectories crossed between June and July, and by September mean NDVI was higher in the stable meadow than in the unstable meadow (0.422 and 0.348, respectively). Across the four surveys, the mean rate of increase was approximately 0.074 NDVI units month\(^{-1}\) in the stable meadow and 0.029 NDVI units month\(^{-1}\) in the unstable meadow. The seasonal increase was therefore approximately 2.6 times greater in the stable meadow, whereas NDVI increased more gradually in the unstable meadow.

Within the stable meadow, mean NDVI increased in both experimental groups, but the increase was more pronounced in treatment cores. Mean NDVI in treatment cores rose from 0.193 in June to 0.454 in September, corresponding to an average increase of 0.088 NDVI units month\(^{-1}\). Over the same period, mean NDVI in control cores increased from 0.213 to 0.389, at an average rate of 0.060 NDVI units month\(^{-1}\). The rate of increase was therefore approximately 1.5 times greater in treatment cores. Treatment values exceeded control values from July onwards, and the difference between the two groups increased towards September.

In the unstable meadow, control and treatment cores exhibited contrasting short-term trajectories but similar overall rates of seasonal increase. Mean NDVI in control cores decreased from 0.279 in June to 0.243 in July, whereas treatment cores showed no corresponding decline and increased from 0.244 to 0.305. Treatment NDVI then remained nearly unchanged between July and August (0.305 and 0.296, respectively), while control NDVI recovered to 0.317. By September, mean NDVI was similar in control and treatment cores (0.354 and 0.342, respectively). Across the complete series, the average rates of increase were 0.030 NDVI units month\(^{-1}\) in control cores and 0.028 NDVI units month\(^{-1}\) in treatment cores.
